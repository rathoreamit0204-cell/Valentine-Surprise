From a Hello… to Forever

Some stories are written by destiny.

It started with a simple hello.

No big promises. No dramatic music.

Just two people who didn’t know
they were about to become each other’s favorite person.

💬 You are my…?

🏡 Home

Because no matter where I am… I feel complete with you.

🌙 Peace

You calm storms I don’t even talk about.

🔥 Strength

You make me want to become a better man.

💫 Favorite Person

The first one I want to tell everything to.

🌍 Future

I don’t imagine tomorrow without you in it.

❤️ Safe Place

Where my guard drops and my heart stays.

😌 Happiness

My mood changes when your name pops up.

💍 Forever

Not temporary. Not seasonal. Always.

Are you sure that’s all you are?
